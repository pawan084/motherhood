What it is

What Aira is

Aira is an AI maternal-wellness companion. A learner tells Aira where she is in her journey — trying to conceive, pregnant, or postpartum — and Aira shapes a proactive daily home, a gated AI conversation, week-by-week journey guidance, care reminders, and short own-hosted videos around that stage, in English, Hindi, or Hinglish. It offers wellness support, never diagnosis; anything that looks like a danger sign escalates to a human pathway instead of an AI answer.

Two clients, one backend. The web SPA is the reference implementation; Android is the lead client, verified on hardware, built with zero third-party dependencies (framework HTTP, JSON, VideoView, AlarmManager, RemoteViews throughout).

Non-negotiables

Safety gate on every input

The server-side gate screens every chat input before any reply exists — typed or spoken. It is fail-closed: if the gate errors, no AI reply may be generated. A red-flag input gets no AI answer at all — only the full-screen Urgent Help handoff with pathways to human care.

Honest UI

No fake data anywhere. Trust labels reflect real gate results; star ratings are real aggregates; a video with no file shows an honest empty state rather than pretending to be playable.

Learner data is owner-scoped

Every table is owned by the learner: exported on request, deletable, and merged when a guest signs in. Every new resource must wire into merge / erase / export before it ships. The aggregate metrics table has no learner column by construction.

Never used for advertising

Sensitive health data is not an ad product. AI personalisation is a consent with real effect: switched off, no memories enter prompts and no extraction runs.

Features — safety, conversation, and the proactive home

Safety & trust
  • Clinical safety gate screening every turn (Gemini safety model), fail-closed with an honest "safety check unavailable" state

  • AI disclaimer served by the backend and surfaced beside AI replies
Chat & AI
  • Gated AI turns with empathy-first replies and typed content cards
  • SSE streaming on web; blocking POST /respond on Android

Design & status

Design system — the "Dawn" direction, shipped

Aira's visual language pairs an editorial serif (Fraunces-class) with a calm plum/lilac/sage palette. The Dawn redesign — proposed in an HTML before/after kit and then implemented move by move in the Compose app — made six moves:

1 · Twilight → Dawn

The dark hero becomes a light sunrise gradient (peach → lilac) with plum ink; the dark theme keeps its dark hero for 3 AM feeds.

2 · Shadows replace borders

Cards, bubbles, and tiles drop 1px outlines for soft plum-tinted shadows and larger radii.

3 · A warm second accent

Rose joins plum on eyebrows, tool tiles, and category chips; sage keeps meaning "done / safe".

4 · Frosted nav

The floating nav pill goes translucent white with a solid plum active tab.

5 · Editorial serif

Italics for greetings and emphasis words; the welcome headline grows. Companion voice, not system chrome.

6 · Rounder controls

Buttons, options, segments, mood chips, and check buttons go full-pill or circle; the primary button gains a plum gradient.

The design team works from docs/design/index.html (all 13 screens as living HTML, token-driven) and docs/design/redesign.html (before/after pairs) — editing one CSS token block re-skins every screen.
